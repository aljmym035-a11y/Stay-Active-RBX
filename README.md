Welcome to **AFK Guardian Sentinel**, a thoughtfully engineered presence-continuity utility crafted for players who want their Roblox sessions to remain warm, stable, and uninterrupted while they step away for tea, a meeting, homework, or simply a breath of real-world air. Rather than treating absence as a problem to be brute-forced, AFK Guardian Sentinel treats it as a natural rhythm of play — and politely keeps the rhythm going.

This project is the spiritual successor to a lineage of lightweight anti-AFK scripts, rebuilt from the ground up with a modern, responsive interface, multilingual awareness, and a relentless commitment to stability. It is *not* a shortcut around the rules — it is a session companion that respects the platform, respects the player, and respects the clock ticking in the background.

## 🌟 Why AFK Guardian Sentinel Exists

There is a peculiar anxiety that comes with stepping away from a game session. You close the laptop lid for five minutes and return to find yourself unceremoniously ejected, a spectator to your own progress. AFK Guardian Sentinel was born from that anxiety — and from the belief that a small, careful piece of software could quietly solve it.

The name is deliberate. A sentinel does not shout; it watches. A guardian does not intrude; it protects. This tool sits in the corner of your session like a night-shift lighthouse keeper, rotating its beam, ensuring the flame never goes out.

## ✨ Feature Constellation

AFK Guardian Sentinel bundles a constellation of capabilities, each designed to feel like a small kindness rather than a technicality.

### 🖥️ Responsive User Interface
The control surface adapts fluidly from a sprawling ultrawide monitor down to a modest laptop screen. Panels reflow, typography scales, and the whole experience feels native regardless of your display. The UI leans on a calm, low-contrast palette because your session companion should never compete with the game itself.

### 🌍 Multilingual Support
With locale packs spanning more than forty languages — from English and Spanish to Japanese, Turkish, Polish, Vietnamese, and beyond — the interface speaks your language before you have to. Translations are community-refined and reviewed for tone, not just accuracy.

### ☎️ 24/7 Customer Support
A round-the-clock support channel stands ready. Whether it is a configuration question at 3 a.m. or a subtle bug report at noon, the Sentinel crew responds. Real humans, real answers, no labyrinth of automated replies.

### 🧠 Smart Presence Scheduling
Define active hours, idle thresholds, and quiet windows. The Sentinel respects your calendar as much as your game.

### 🎛️ Per-Session Profiles
Different games demand different rhythms. Save distinct profiles and swap between them in a single click.

### 🔔 Gentle Notification Layer
Optional, non-intrusive pings let you know when a session has been preserved or when your attention is needed — nothing more, nothing less.

### 📊 Session Insight Dashboard
A tidy analytics panel shows uptime, continuity events, and historical trends, so you can see exactly how much session life the Sentinel has safeguarded.

### 🔒 Local-First Privacy Posture
Configuration lives on your machine. Nothing is uploaded, syndicated, or shared. The Sentinel works in the quiet of your own environment.

### ⚙️ Configuration Portability
Export and import your settings as a single, human-readable file — perfect for moving between devices or restoring after a reinstall.

### 🧩 Modular Extension Hooks
Advanced users can attach small behavior modules to the Sentinel’s event pipeline, tailoring it without forking the core.

## 🎯 Design Philosophy

AFK Guardian Sentinel is shaped by four principles:

1. **Restraint** — The tool does its job and then gets out of the way.
2. **Transparency** — Every action is logged, every setting is visible, nothing hides in the shadows.
3. **Resilience** — Sessions break; the Sentinel bends, recovers, and continues.
4. **Respect** — For the player, for the platform, and for the community that keeps the lights on.

## 🚀 A Walkthrough of Your First Ten Minutes

When you launch AFK Guardian Sentinel for the first time, a warm onboarding flow greets you.

- **Step One — Locale.** Choose your language. The interface reskins instantly.
- **Step Two — Profile.** Pick a preset: “Casual,” “Long Session,” “Study Break,” or “Custom.”
- **Step Three — Timing.** Set the idle threshold. The default of a few minutes suits most players, but you are free to nudge it in either direction.
- **Step Four — Notifications.** Choose how chatty the Sentinel should be.
- **Step Five — Confirmation.** A short summary screen reveals exactly what will happen. You press the final button. The Sentinel begins to watch.

From that point forward, the experience is largely invisible. You play. You step away. You come back. Nothing has changed — which is precisely the point.

## 🛠️ Configuration Deep Dive

Configuration is where the Sentinel reveals its depth. The settings file is organized into neat sections:

- **Session** — thresholds, retry cadence, and reconnection strategy.
- **Interface** — theme, density, and locale.
- **Notifications** — channel selection, verbosity, and quiet hours.
- **Profiles** — named bundles of the above, each independently editable.
- **Advanced** — extension hooks, logging verbosity, and experimental toggles.

Every field is documented in-app with a short tooltip, and the full reference lives in the docs folder of the repository.

## 🔐 Privacy, Ethics, and Boundaries

AFK Guardian Sentinel takes a firm stance on boundaries. It is a session-stability tool, and only that. It does not interact with game economies, does not simulate human behavior beyond the minimum required for session continuity, and does not collect telemetry of any kind. Configuration data is stored locally. Network activity is limited to the Sentinel’s own update checks, which can be disabled entirely.

We believe that tools should be legible. Every log line the Sentinel produces is human-readable. Nothing obfuscated, nothing hidden.
